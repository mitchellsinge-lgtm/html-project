EV charger installation: your questions answered

Straight answers, no jargon.

How much does EV charger installation cost in Brisbane?

Our EV charger packages are supplied and installed: $1,299 for a 7kW single-phase charger and $1,799 for a 22kW three-phase charger (E Series charger). That is standard installation only, and additional electrical work or switchboard upgrades are quoted separately if required. If you already have a charger, installation only starts from $930 for single phase or $1,100 for three phase. Every job includes a dedicated circuit, safety switch and Certificate of Compliance. Free quote, no pressure.

Do I need a licensed electrician to install an EV charger in Queensland?

Yes. Any electrical work in Queensland, including a new dedicated circuit for an EV charger, must be done by a licensed electrical contractor. Mitch is a licensed electrical contractor (Licence No. 1508151) and issues a Certificate of Compliance when the job is done.

Which EV chargers and cars do you install for?

We install wall-mounted 7kW home chargers for Tesla, BYD, Polestar, MG and all other brands, and we explain how the charger and app work before we leave.

Single phase or three phase: which do I need?

Most homes run a single phase supply, which is fine for overnight home charging. Three phase can charge faster if your home is wired for it. We check your switchboard on the free quote and recommend the right option.

Which Brisbane suburbs do you cover?

We are based in Greenslopes and Salisbury and cover central and south Brisbane, Logan and the Gold Coast, as well as north Brisbane.
