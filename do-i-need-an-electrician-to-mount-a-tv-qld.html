Do I need an electrician to mount a TV in Queensland?

By Massive Integrated Homes · Licensed electrical contractor, Lic. No. 1508151 · Updated November 2026

It depends on what the job involves. Hanging a TV on a wall bracket is not electrical work, but anything that touches mains power is, and in Queensland that must be done by a licensed electrician.

What you can usually do without an electrician

  • Fixing a bracket to the wall and hanging the TV.
  • Plugging the TV and devices into an existing power point.
  • Running low-voltage cables such as HDMI along the wall in a cable cover.

What needs a licensed electrician

  • Adding a new power point behind or near the TV.
  • Moving an existing power point.
  • Running power cable inside the wall so nothing shows below the TV.
  • Recessing a power and HDMI box behind the screen for a clean finish.

If you are not sure which side of the line your job falls on, check with Queensland's Electrical Safety Office or ask a licensed electrician before work starts.

Why it matters

Unlicensed electrical work is illegal in Queensland, can void your home insurance, and can be dangerous. A licensed electrician also issues the compliance paperwork if new or moved power points are installed.
