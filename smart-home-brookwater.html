One home. One app. No lock-in.

Most smart homes end up as a pile of separate apps that don’t talk to each other. We bring them together on MiHub, a compact hub in your home that connects the devices you already own and the ones you add later, from different brands. That means you can start small, add rooms over time, and avoid being tied to one manufacturer’s hardware. Voice control works through Google Assistant or Alexa, and you can reach the system securely when you’re away.

Brookwater smart home questions

How much does smart home automation cost in Brookwater?

It depends on the size of the home and how much you want to control. Our packages are Essentials at $3,990, Connected at $8,990 and Intelligent from $4,990 plus $39 a month. We give you a fixed quote after a free on-site consultation.

Do I need to replace my existing devices?

Usually not. MiHub connects devices from many brands, so we work with what you already have and only recommend new hardware where it genuinely helps.

When should I plan the smart home in a new build?

Before the frame is lined, ideally at the electrical design stage. That's when cabling, lighting circuits and speaker positions are cheapest to place.

Can I control it by voice?

Yes. Voice control works through Google Assistant or Alexa, alongside the main app.

Who looks after it after installation?

Essentials includes 30 days of setup support and Connected includes 6 months. The Intelligent package includes ongoing remote support and updates. You can reach Nate or Mitch directly on 0480 568 180.

Planning a build or renovation?

The best time to plan your smart home is before the walls close. We work with Brookwater builders and renovators to place cabling, lighting circuits, speaker positions and the hub while the frame is still open, which costs far less than retrofitting. If you’re working with a builder, we’re happy to speak to them directly.
