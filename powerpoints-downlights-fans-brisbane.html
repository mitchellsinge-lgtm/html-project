How much does a new powerpoint cost in Brisbane?

Powerpoints start from $229, supplied, fitted and tested. Indoor, outdoor and USB powerpoints are available. New circuits or hard-to-reach locations are quoted separately.

How much do LED downlights cost to install?

From $79 per light, replacing old halogens with dimmable LEDs. The final price depends on how many lights and the ceiling access.

How much does ceiling fan installation cost?

From $249 at an existing wired location, with DC-motor, remote and smart-home-ready fans available. A new fan where there is no wiring needs a quote.

Do I need an electrician to fit a powerpoint or fan?

Yes. Wiring powerpoints, lights and fans is licensed electrical work in Queensland.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Massive Integrated Homes holds Electrical Contractor Licence No. 1508151.
