Floating TV cabinets: your questions answered

Straight answers, no jargon.

What's included in the floating cabinet and TV wall mount package?

We supply the floating cabinet, mount your TV on the wall, mount your soundbar, install a recessed power point and HDMI behind the TV, and hide the cables between the TV and the cabinet. One team does the joinery install and the electrical in the same job.

How much does a floating TV cabinet with wall mount cost?

It depends on the cabinet length, finish, your TV size and your wall type, so every package is quoted after we see the space. Quotes are free, with no obligation. Send us a photo of your wall and the TV size to get started.

What finishes are available?

Timber, white and black finishes, as shown in our recent installs. Tell us the look you're after and we'll match the cabinet to your room.

Why do I need a licensed electrician for this?

Adding a power point behind the TV is licensed electrical work in Queensland. Because we're licensed electrical contractors (Lic. 1508151), the recessed power, HDMI and hidden cabling are done properly in the same visit as the cabinet and TV.

Can the cabinet go on any wall?

Most walls are suitable, including plasterboard, timber-framed and brick. We check the wall first and choose fixings to suit the cabinet and what you'll store in it.

Can you mount my soundbar too?

Yes. Soundbar mounting is included, so the TV, soundbar and cabinet line up neatly with no visible cables.

Which areas do you cover?

Brisbane and Logan. We're based in Greenslopes and Salisbury and regularly work across central and south Brisbane.
